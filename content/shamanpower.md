### What it is
ShamanPower is an all-in-one totem addon for shamans. It replaces a handful of separate tools with one interface for totems, cooldowns and raid utility, and it comes with a guided setup and a searchable settings window.

**[Download ShamanPower on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/shamanpower)** or **[join the addon's Discord server](https://discord.gg/btzwXUuqa)**. Questions, bugs or ideas for the addon itself go there.

It started as a shaman take on PallyPower, written by taubut, who later rewrote it from the ground up. It is now maintained by the ShamanPower Team.

### What it does
- **Totem bar:** Several display styles, a flyout menu for each element, duration and pulse timers, a cast-all button and fade rules.
- **Cooldown bar:** Tracks shields, weapon imbues and abilities such as Ankh, Nature's Swiftness and Mana Tide.
- **Loadouts:** Save up to 8 totem sets and switch between them by hand, or automatically by the kind of content you are in.
- **Party buff tracker:** Shows which party members currently have your totem buffs.
- **Ready reminders:** Icons you can place anywhere that show when a spell comes off cooldown.
- **Raid tools:** Assign each shaman's totems and the cooldown callers. Callers can be any class.
- **Totem range overlay:** Shows whether any class is standing in range of a totem.
- **Windfury tracking:** Follows melee Windfury and warns about expiring buffs and threats.
- **Everything else:** Eight languages, font and sound options, and a keybind mode.

### Where it runs
One download covers WoW: Forever and TBC Anniversary.
