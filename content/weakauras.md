### What these are
These are the WeakAuras I have built for shamans, published on Wago.

**[Browse my WeakAuras on Wago](https://wago.io/p/miska.)**

Many of them have already been adapted into [ShamanPower](shamanpower.html), so some of what you see there started life as one of these auras.

### The main one
The WeakAura most people will have used is **Stormbringer's Handbook: S.H.O.C.K**.

If you know my WeakAuras by one name, this is it. Its features are the ones you are most likely to recognise in ShamanPower.

### Before you use them
- **Probably in the addon already:** Many of them have been adapted into ShamanPower. Any that are not in the addon yet will most likely be adapted into it.
- **Missing one?:** If there is something you want that is not in the addon yet, come to the [addon's Discord server](https://discord.gg/btzwXUuqa) and give us feedback.
