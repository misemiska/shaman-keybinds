### The build
My talent order for leveling and dungeons, from level 10 to 60. It ends with 26 points in Enhancement, 17 in Elemental and 8 in Restoration.

Scroll down and the talent trees on the right fill in, one point per level, as each part is explained. You can also drag the level slider above the trees.

### Levels 10 to 20
This build has Improved Ghost Wolf ready for level 20, when you get Ghost Wolf. One point goes into Improved Lightning Shield first, so you do not sit on two points in Improved Ghost Wolf before you even have the ability.

| Levels | Talent | Ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 to 14 | Thundering Strikes | 1 to 5 |
| 15 to 17 | Mental Dexterity | 1 to 3 |
| 18 | Improved Lightning Shield | 1 |
| 19 to 20 | Improved Ghost Wolf | 1 to 2 |

### Levels 21 to 32
Shamanistic Focus comes next. Anticipation is taken now to prepare for Improved Stormstrike resets. Then Stormstrike, then Improved Stormstrike, and finally Spirit Weapons for the parry.

| Levels | Talent | Ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | Shamanistic Focus | 1 |
| 22 to 24 | Anticipation | 1 to 3 |
| 25 | Stormstrike | 1 |
| 26 to 29 | Flurry | 1 to 4 |
| 30 to 31 | Improved Stormstrike | 1 to 2 |
| 32 | Spirit Weapons | 1 |

### Levels 33 to 40
We leave the Enhancement tree for Tidal Focus and Mindfulness.

| Levels | Talent | Ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 33 to 37 | Tidal Focus | 1 to 5 |
| 38 to 40 | Mindfulness | 1 to 3 |

### Levels 41 to 57
After that, the Elemental tree, for improved fire totems (Call of Flame), Clearcasting (Elemental Focus) and Elemental Devastation, for the fun.

**Elemental Devastation and Call of Flame can be taken in either order.** Pick whichever you want first with the switch below, and the table and the talent trees swap with it. Elemental Focus stays at level 51 either way.

| 46 to 48 | Elemental Devastation | 1 to 3 |
| 49 to 50 | Call of Flame | 1 to 2 |
| 51 | Elemental Focus | 1 |
| 52 | Call of Flame | 3 |
| 53 to 55 | Concussion | 1 to 3 |
| 56 to 57 | Trial by Fire | 1 to 2 |
