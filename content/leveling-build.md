### The build
My talent order for leveling and dungeons, from level 10 to 60. It ends with 24 points in Enhancement, 19 in Elemental and 8 in Restoration.

Scroll down and the talent trees on the right fill in, one point per level, as each part is explained. You can also drag the level slider above the trees.

### Levels 10 to 19
This build has Improved Ghost Wolf finished at level 19, ready for level 20, when you get Ghost Wolf.

| Levels | Talent | Ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 to 14 | Thundering Strikes | 1 to 5 |
| 15 to 17 | Mental Dexterity | 1 to 3 |
| 18 to 19 | Improved Ghost Wolf | 1 to 2 |

### Levels 20 to 32
Shamanistic Focus comes first, then one point in Elemental Weapons. Anticipation is taken now to prepare for Improved Stormstrike resets. Then Stormstrike, then Improved Stormstrike, and finally Spirit Weapons for the parry.

| Levels | Talent | Ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | Shamanistic Focus | 1 |
| 21 | Elemental Weapons | 1 |
| 22 to 24 | Anticipation | 1 to 3 |
| 25 | Stormstrike | 1 |
| 26 to 29 | Flurry | 1 to 4 |
| 30 to 31 | Improved Stormstrike | 1 to 2 |
| 32 | Spirit Weapons | 1 |

### Levels 33 to 40

### Levels 41 to 57
After that, the Elemental tree, for Concussion, improved fire totems (Call of Flame), Clearcasting (Elemental Focus), Reverberation and Elemental Devastation, for the fun.

**Elemental Devastation and Call of Flame can be taken in either order.** Pick whichever you want first with the switch below, and the table and the talent trees swap with it. Elemental Focus stays at level 51 either way.
